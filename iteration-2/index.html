§ 01

A note from the founder.

Aklan raised me. The women here — my mother first among them — never spoke of beauty as vanity. It was care, kept quietly. A drop of coconut oil. A mother's hand on a tense shoulder. The discipline of going to bed on time. When I returned home with a medical degree and a board certification in anesthesiology, I wanted to honour that lineage with the precision my training demanded. Aurum is the result.

Every injection here begins with a conversation. Every drip with a screening. Every facial in a room that smells faintly of lemongrass and clean cotton. We open at nine because that is when the light is best, and we close at eight because nobody should be receiving Botox at midnight.

If we suit you, you will know within the first hour. If we do not, our concierge will gladly recommend three other places — including one in Iloilo and one in Manila. Beauty is not a transaction. It is a relationship.

— Dr. Josefa A. Magallanes, M.D., DPBA

§ 05 · Profile

The doctor who came home.

Dr. Josefa A. Magallanes — Berry, to those who know her — trained as an anesthesiologist at Asia Pacific Medical Center, where she still practices. She returned to Kalibo not because she had to, but because she chose to. Aurum was opened in 2026, on a quiet street in Poblacion, with three rules nailed above the door:

  1. Nothing is administered without a screening.
  2. Nothing is sold that the doctor would not recommend to her sister.
  3. Nothing is rushed.

Her clinical specialty — pain management — informs every aesthetic decision. She is the woman who knows where the needles should not go, and that knowledge shows in faces that look like themselves, only better-rested.

§ 09

Practical matters.

Is every aesthetic procedure performed by a doctor?

Yes. Every injection, thread, and laser is performed or directly supervised by Dr. Josefa A. Magallanes. The aestheticians on our team handle facials, peels, and massage; medical procedures are doctor-only.

How early should I book before my Boracay trip?

For facials and IV drips, same-day booking is usually fine. For Botox and fillers we recommend at least seven days — minor swelling and bruising should settle before any photographs are taken.

Do you offer wedding or couple packages?

Bridal trial programmes begin three months before the date and may include glow-up facials, IV drip cycles, and an optional pre-wedding Botox consultation. Speak to our concierge to plan a custom carte.

What payment methods are accepted?

Cash, GCash, Maya, and major credit/debit cards. Gold Circle members are billed monthly. Package purchases may be split across two visits.

Are walk-ins accepted?

Yes, subject to availability. We prefer reservations for doctor-led services, which require a brief consultation slot.
