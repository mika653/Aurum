A Note from the Founder

Care, kept quietly.

Aurum was built around the women of Aklan — first my mother, then her sisters, then the friends I grew up with — and the way they had always practiced beauty as a quiet duty. A bottle of coconut oil. A massage at the edge of the kitchen. A plant pressed onto a tense shoulder. Care, kept quietly.

What I added to that lineage was the precision of medicine. I am Dr. Josefa A. Magallanes, M.D., DPBA — a board-certified Anesthesiologist at Asia Pacific Medical Center, Aklan. Every aesthetic procedure here is performed or directly supervised by me. The rest of the carte — facials, peels, massage, the rituals — is offered by aestheticians I have personally trained, in rooms I have personally cleaned.

If we suit you, you'll know within the first hour. We'd like very much to welcome you home.

Dr. Berry Dr. Josefa A. Magallanes, M.D., DPBA · Founder & Medical Director

Practical Matters

Common questions.

Are the Mother's Day specials really doctor-supervised?

Yes. Even the most affordable promo (Smoother & Lifted Glow at ₱580) is performed in our clinical-grade rooms with Dr. Magallanes on-premises. Promotional pricing does not change our standards.

Until when can I book the Mother's Day specials?

The promotional pricing is valid through 20 May 2026. Treatments themselves can be redeemed any time within 90 days of purchase, so you can gift now and book the visit later.

What is HIFU treatment and what areas can it treat?

HIFU (High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound) is a non-invasive lift, working at the SMAS layer — the same layer surgeons tighten in a face-lift. Treatable areas include lower face, jawline, neck, brow, and abdomen. From ₱5,380 per area; ₱8,880 for full-face. A 10 ml Aurum Blue Peptide Serum is included.

What is Blackdoll, and how is it different from a regular facial?

Blackdoll is a Carbon Laser Peel — a fine layer of carbon is applied to the skin, then targeted by a Q-switched laser. It exfoliates, brightens, tightens pores, and reduces oiliness in a single session. Especially loved by guests with combination/oily skin.

Can I bring my mother for a same-day Hydrafacial?

Yes. Same-day Hydrafacial bookings (including the Aurum Hydrafacial for 2 with 24K Gold mask) are usually available. Please call ahead so we can hold a paired room if you'd like to be treated side-by-side.

Where does the “Aurum 24K Gold” product line come from?

It is our in-house formulation, produced in small batches by a partner laboratory in Manila, made specifically for our facials. The 24K Gold Serum and Jelly Mask are not sold separately — they're included in our Aurum Hydrafacial and select promotions.
