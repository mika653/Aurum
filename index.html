Questions, Answered

Before You Book

The questions our guests ask most often — answered candidly by Dr. Magallanes and our front-of-house team.

Is Aurum a doctor-led aesthetic clinic?

Yes. Every aesthetic procedure at Aurum is performed or directly supervised by Dr. Josefa A. Magallanes, M.D., DPBA — a board-certified Anesthesiologist and trained aesthetician. Her medical background means we approach injectables, laser, and PRP with the same patient-safety standards you'd find in a hospital setting.

Do you offer Botox and dermal fillers in Kalibo, Aklan?

Yes — FDA-approved Botox for forehead lines, crow's feet, and frown lines, and hyaluronic-acid dermal fillers for lips, cheeks, chin, jawline, and nasolabial folds. All injections are performed by Dr. Magallanes herself, after a complimentary consultation to map a plan that suits your face.

How much does IV Drip Therapy cost, and is it safe?

Drips start at ₱1,500. Standard Glutathione, Vitamin C, and immunity drips are available, with premium Korean glutathione formulations on request. Every drip begins with a brief medical screening — vitals, allergy review, medication check — and is administered by trained staff under physician oversight.

I'm flying into Kalibo for Boracay — can I squeeze in a treatment?

Absolutely. We're a five-minute drive from Kalibo Airport and open daily 9 AM – 8 PM, weekends included. Travelers most often book the Post-Flight Recovery package (₱1,200, 90 minutes) or a Hydrafacial before the ferry. Tell us your flight time when booking and we'll hold a window.

What is Hilot, and how is it different from Swedish massage?

Hilot is a traditional Filipino healing art that uses banana leaves placed on the body for diagnosis (cooler areas indicate tension or imbalance) and virgin coconut oil for warm, therapeutic strokes. Where Swedish massage is rhythmic and broadly relaxing, Hilot is diagnostic and intuitive — a heritage practice we honour at clinical-grade hygiene standards.

Do I need to take time off work after Botox or fillers?

No. Most guests return to work the same day. You may have small pinpoint marks at injection sites that resolve within an hour, and minor bruising is possible with fillers. Avoid strenuous exercise, alcohol, and lying flat for four hours after Botox to keep the product where Dr. Magallanes placed it.

Are walk-ins accepted?

Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability, but we recommend booking online — especially for doctor-led procedures, which require a consultation slot. Our online calendar shows live availability against existing appointments.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, GCash, Maya, and major credit/debit cards. Gold Circle members are billed monthly; package purchases can be split across two visits on request.
