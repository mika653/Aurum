The Aurum protocol

Six steps. Every guest. Every time.

No procedure is started without all six. There are no fast lanes — including for friends of the house.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    20 minutes. Medical history, photographs, expectations. Always complimentary, always private.

  2. 02

    Screening

    Vitals, allergy review, medication check, contraindication assessment. Documented and timestamped.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Written treatment plan with exact dosages, anatomical maps, and a per-area cost breakdown. You take it home.

  4. 04

    Procedure

    Performed by Dr. Magallanes in our clinical suite. Local anaesthesia and ice as appropriate. Time-of-day matched to your schedule.

  5. 05

    Recovery

    15–60 minute on-site monitoring per procedure type. Aftercare kit and a printed schedule for the first 14 days.

  6. 06

    Follow-up

    Mandatory check-in at the 14-day mark. Photographs are compared, dosage notes are kept, and any touch-up sits inside the original quote.

Common questions

Practical, candid answers.

What clinical safety standards does Aurum follow?

Hospital-grade hygiene. FDA-approved products only. Pre-procedure screening for every guest. Emergency kit on-premises (oxygen, epinephrine, defibrillator). Documented audit trail kept for seven years.

What is your published success rate?

Across 1,200+ procedures: 96% patient-reported satisfaction. 0.4% adverse-event rate (any degree). 0.0% serious-adverse-event rate. We publish quarterly.

How is Aurum different from a cosmetic spa?

A spa is staffed by aestheticians; medical procedures rely on visiting physicians or unclear supervision. Aurum is a medical clinic — every aesthetic procedure is performed or directly supervised by Dr. Magallanes on-premises.

Can I bring my own products or skincare regimen?

Yes. Bring everything. We will review against the procedure timeline and advise on what to pause (typically retinoids 5 days before injectables; AHAs 7 days before chemical peels).

Do you treat men?

Yes. Approximately 18% of our injectable patients are men. Most begin with a single-session masseter or jaw-edge plan; some return for low-dose Botox between brows. Discreet, photographed, and kept entirely off social media unless you ask.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations 12+ hours in advance: complimentary. Less than 12 hours: 50% of session fee held against next booking. No-shows: full session fee held. Gold Circle members: 4 short-notice cancellations per year, no fee.
